This program is designed to allow the user to enter in financial data and turn it into an xlsx document that they can then use for future reference.
As of now, there are seven features that have been implemented into this program.
1. Investment calculator: This feature takes a base investment, interest rate, and added growth and determines how much the person who invested in the account will have after 40 years, or 480 months, if they continue at their current rate. This calculator only deals with positive investments.
2. Budget Calculator: This feature takes a list of budget information provided by the user and in turn shows them how much many they have left over. The feature also gives them a frugal budget for them if they want to pay off their debts faster, as well as a more lavish budget if they want to spend more. This feature also produces pie charts to show the user how much they spend by category.'
3. College calculator: This feature takes an initial investment, interest rate, and either a goal or a monthly investment (that can be 0). Then, it provides the user with an excel document that covers eighteen years (216 months) and shows them how they would go to either reach their goal, or to determine how much will be in the account after each month. The goal with this calculator is to prepare parents to invest for their children's education. The assumption is that the money is placed in before the child is born.
4. Loan calculator: This feature takes an initial loan, interest rate, and pay per month. From there, it tells the user how many months it will take for them to pay off their loan, or whether they will even pay it off at all. It also lets the user know how much true money they are paying, as many people aren't aware of how much money they truly spend when getting a 20,000 dollar car loan.
5. Retirement Goal Calculator: This feature takes current savings, the needs that a person will need in retirement per month, the growth rate, their current age, their planned retirement age, and their expected death age. From there, the user receives an excel file with three columns. One column shows how much they need to save to have a retirement account that lasts until their death plus five years, one that shows how much they need to save to have a maintenance account, and one that shows how much they need to save to take out half of the growth.
6. Retirement Duration Calculator: This feature takes current savings, pension, social security, monthly needs, expected years left, and growth rate. From there, it gives them either a document that shows how long their money will last and what it will be at their death, or a document that shows the maximum monthly amount that they can take out safely.
7. Take Home Pay Calculator: This feature uses pay rate per period, tax rate, and set aside money. It then returns a document containing their pay for that period and what portion goes to each source. This feature is not yet finished.
8. Home Affordability Calculator: This feature uses uses the current monthly pay rate, house interest rate, down payment, and current monthly debt payments and provides the user how much home they can afford based on how much of their current monthly pay they are willing to pay towards a new house.
